## LEAN 4 PROOF EXPLANATION: AND INTRODUCTION
Theorem: (P : Prop) → (Q : Prop) → P → Q → P ∧ Q

CODE:
theorem and_intro (P Q : Prop) (hp : P) (hq : Q) : P ∧ Q := by
constructor
· exact hp
· exact hq

STEP-BY-STEP BREAKDOWN:

1. "constructor"

* What it does: Since our goal is an AND statement ($P \land Q$), the `constructor` tactic splits the single goal into two separate sub-goals: one to prove the left side ($P$) and one to prove the right side ($Q$).

2. FIRST SUB-GOAL: `· exact hp`

* What it does: The current sub-goal is to prove $P$. We use `exact hp` to fulfill this goal directly using the hypothesis `hp` that was provided in the theorem arguments.

3. SECOND SUB-GOAL: `· exact hq`

* What it does: The current sub-goal is to prove $Q$. We use `exact hq` to fulfill this goal directly using the hypothesis `hq` provided in the theorem arguments.

KEY TAKEAWAYS / CORRECTIONS:

* `constructor` targets conjunctive goals, breaking an AND statement into its component parts so you can prove them independently.
* The dot bullets (`·`) are used in Lean 4 to structure and isolate proofs for individual sub-goals.
* *(Alternative)* You can also prove this in a single line using anonymous constructor notation without tactics: `exact ⟨hp, hq⟩`.
